The actual ESP32 CPU and firmware simulation is provided by Wokwi. This studio prepares your sketch and a Wokwi-compatible circuit export; it does not pretend that a drawing canvas executes ESP32 machine code.

1 · PrepareSave your circuit and firmware here.
2 · ExportDownload the sketch and diagram JSON.
3 · SimulateOpen Wokwi and load your exported files.
Wokwi is an external service. Import compatibility depends on supported component types and pin mappings. Simulation can differ from physical hardware; validate important designs on real boards too.
